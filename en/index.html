ARTICLE Escalation: Same Wall, Higher CostHit the same obstacle three times and raise the cost each time; only then does the character look cornered. ARTICLE A Sequence: Three Scenes, One ChangeOne scene delivers one change; three scenes together move a sequence. ARTICLE Midpoint Turn: Change the Method, Not the GoalThe midpoint is not a plot twist; it is the character changing methods to pursue the same want. ARTICLE Ending: Stop After the ChoiceEnd after the choice. The sentence the audience supplies is stronger than a closing explanation.
